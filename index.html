About the studio

Small by design. Focused in execution.

Lucky Wise Games is an independent game studio focused on direct, memorable gameplay and carefully built atmosphere.

We take inspiration from classic games without being bound to a single genre. Every project is approached as a chance to refine systems, pacing and the feeling of being present in a game world.
