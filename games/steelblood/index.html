About the game

A classic spirit. A new mission.

Steelblood: Allied Front is an old-school first-person shooter set during World War II, inspired by classics such as Medal of Honor and Half-Life.

Built around a story-driven single-player campaign, the game combines direct FPS combat with large battles, infiltration and sabotage across connected chapters.

The campaign

Fight. Infiltrate. Sabotage.

Take the role of an OSS agent in a story-driven campaign built around frontline combat, covert infiltration and sabotage.

Each operation carries the story directly into the next, keeping the campaign focused and continuous while shifting naturally between large-scale battles and missions behind enemy lines.
